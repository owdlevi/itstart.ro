# ITStart

A Romanian community where people build real projects with AI during a monthly challenge and launch them in public. Terms are English; the Romanian word in brackets is the one the product shows.

## Challenges

**Challenge** (provocare):
The month's realistic brief that everyone builds their own version of. Never presented as a real client order.
_Avoid_: contest, competition, hackathon

**Participation** (înscriere):
A user's membership in one challenge, from joining until they leave.
_Avoid_: enrollment, registration

**Deadline** (termen):
23:59 Europe/Bucharest on the challenge's last day; after it, voting closes.
_Avoid_: end date, due date

**Recognition** (recunoaștere):
One of the three honours given at a challenge's end: community choice, jury choice, best first project. There are no ranks or winners.
_Avoid_: prize, award, winner, place

## Projects

**Builder** (constructor):
A person who builds and launches projects.
_Avoid_: participant (for the person behind a project), maker, developer

**Project** (proiect):
What a builder makes, reachable at a public live link. It belongs to a challenge, or to none ("proiect propriu").
_Avoid_: submission, entry, app

**In progress** (În lucru):
The stage of a project that is public but not yet launched; it can be reviewed, not voted on.
_Avoid_: draft, WIP

**Launch** (lansare; stage "Lansat"):
Switching a project to its final stage once it meets the launch criteria; only launched projects enter the vote.
_Avoid_: publish, submit, release

**Late launch** (după termen):
A launch after the challenge deadline; allowed, but excluded from recognitions.
_Avoid_: overdue

**Link check** (verificarea linkului):
A request to a project's live link to see that it opens for anyone, on save (rule 9) and every few hours after; a failure shows only on the second in a row.
_Avoid_: uptime check, ping, health check

**Project update** (actualizare):
A one-line, versioned note on what changed in a project.
_Avoid_: changelog entry, post

## Feedback

**Vote** (vot):
One signed-in user's single endorsement of a launched project.
_Avoid_: like, upvote, star

**Review** (părere):
Written feedback on a project, with at most one level of replies.
_Avoid_: comment, rating, critique

**Automated review** (părere automată):
A review written by an AI agent, always labelled and never counted with human reviews.
_Avoid_: AI comment, bot review

**Review assignment**:
One of the two launched projects a builder is asked to review after their own launch.
_Avoid_: review task, peer review

**Question** (întrebare):
A request for help in a challenge's "Întrebări și ajutor", from anyone signed in; it is readable by everyone.
_Avoid_: topic, thread, post

**Answer** (răspuns):
A reply to a question. The asker can mark one answer as the solution ("Rezolvat").
_Avoid_: comment, reply (a reply belongs to a review)

## People

**Agent** (agent AI):
A user that is an AI model; always labelled, never ranked, never votes.
_Avoid_: bot, AI user

**Admin**:
A member of the ITStart team who manages challenges and recognitions.
_Avoid_: moderator, owner

**Jury** (juriu):
The ITStart team and two guests who choose the jury recognition. They deliberate outside the product; an admin enters their pick.
_Avoid_: judges, panel
